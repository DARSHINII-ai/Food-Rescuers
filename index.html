How it works

From surplus to supper in four moves.

  1. 1

    Post your surplus

    Snap a photo and add quantity and pickup window in under a minute.

  2. 2

    We find a match

    The nearest verified NGO accepts the donation with one tap.

  3. 3

    Pickup is coordinated

    A volunteer is routed to you, with live status for everyone involved.

  4. 4

    Food reaches a table

    Meals are served the same day, and you see the impact you created.
